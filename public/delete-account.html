Delete Account & Associated Data

Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) & GDPR Right to Erasure

⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All your active audiobooks, transcripts, bookshelf records, and stored audio will be immediately and irreversibly erased.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the PaperEcho mobile app installed on your iPhone or Android device:

  1. Open the PaperEcho app.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap "Delete Account & Erase All Data" and confirm.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or lost your device, submit your registered account email below. Our compliance team will process complete data erasure within 24 hours.
