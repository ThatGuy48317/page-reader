Frequently Asked Questions

📸 How should I record my book pages for the best results?

Hold your camera steady in good lighting. Flip through each page smoothly, ensuring the text remains in focus for at least 1–2 seconds per page. The app automatically strips page numbers, headers, and footnotes.

⏳ Why do my audiobooks expire after 7 days?

PaperEcho strictly complies with personal format-shifting legal safe harbors (17 U.S.C. § 107). Ephemeral 7-day retention guarantees that our servers do not permanently warehouse copyrighted text or recordings. You can re-narrate your book at any time by taking a fresh scan.

💳 How do I cancel or modify my subscription?

On iOS: Open your iPhone Settings → tap your Name/Apple ID → tap Subscriptions → select PaperEcho → tap Cancel Subscription.
On Android: Open the Google Play Store → tap your profile icon → tap Payments & subscriptions → tap Subscriptions → cancel PaperEcho.

♿ How do I activate Low Vision & Blind Accessibility Mode?

Go to the Settings tab in the app and toggle on "Low Vision & Blind Accessibility Mode". This activates tactile haptic pulse confirmations for every action, screen reader spoken navigation labels, and specialized tactile reading styles.
