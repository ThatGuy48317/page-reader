How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

To be effective under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your notice must be in writing and include the following:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., Book Title, Author, ISBN).
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with sufficient information for us to locate the item (e.g., specific audiobook identifier or user account reference).
  • Your contact information (name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address).
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Expeditious Removal & Automated Safeguards

Because PaperEcho enforces an ephemeral 7-day retention clock on synthesized audio recordings, files are routinely purged automatically. Furthermore, intermediate video recordings of physical book pages are permanently erased immediately after character extraction. Upon receipt of a valid DMCA notice, any referenced active audio record will be instantly disabled and deleted.

Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that your content was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may send a written Counter-Notification to our Designated Agent containing the elements prescribed by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).
